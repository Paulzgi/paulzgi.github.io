Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This is a personal Google Drive connection used with rclone on my own computer. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With my authorization, rclone can access Google Drive files and file information needed to copy my university files between Google Drive and my computer.

How data is used and stored

The data is used only for my personal file transfers. Copies remain in my Google Drive or on my own devices. The authorization token is stored locally in my rclone configuration.

Sharing

This personal configuration does not sell or share Google Drive data with other people or services.

Removing access

I can remove local copies, delete files from Google Drive, and revoke rclone's access in my Google Account settings.
